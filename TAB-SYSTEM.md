# 📑 Tab System Implementation

## ✨ Feature Overview

Sekarang setiap gift card punya **tab system** biar konten ga perlu scroll panjang-panjang!

### 🎯 Benefits:

- ✅ **No excessive scrolling** - konten terorganisir dalam tabs
- ✅ **Quick navigation** - langsung jump ke section yang mau dilihat
- ✅ **Clean UI** - ga overwhelming, one section at a time
- ✅ **Mobile-friendly** - tabs responsive & touch-optimized

## 🎨 Design Features:

### Tab Buttons:

- Glassmorphism background
- Emoji icons for visual clarity
- Gradient active state (pink/purple)
- Hover effect with lift animation
- Bottom border separator

### Tab Content:

- Fade-in animation on switch
- Smooth transitions
- No content jump
- Full viewport utilization

### Responsive:

- Wrap on small screens
- Touch-friendly size
- Optimized spacing

---

## 🎮 How to Use:

**Desktop:**

- Click tab buttons to switch content

**Mobile:**

- Tap tab buttons (touch-optimized)
- Swipe main cards for different gifts

**Keyboard:**

- Arrow keys ←/→ for card navigation
- Tab key + Enter for tab switching

---

## 💡 Additional Features:
